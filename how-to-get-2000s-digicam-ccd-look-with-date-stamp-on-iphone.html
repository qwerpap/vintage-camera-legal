How to Get the 2000s Digicam (CCD) Look With a Date Stamp on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Graino developer

Short answer. Open Graino and choose Pocket 03, a free camera built around the early-2000s CCD digicam look with an orange date. Switch the date stamp on, press the shutter and save the print.

Step by step

  1. Open Graino and choose Pocket 03Swipe the camera strip to Pocket 03, the CCD digicam body. It is free.
  2. Turn the date stamp onThe date stamp is a 7-segment orange style and can be toggled on or off. Korean, Japanese and Chinese locales show the year first ('26 10 22).
  3. Press the shutterThe digital camera gives an instant review and holds a card of 99 shots.
  4. Open Lab and the rollOpen Lab (top-left) and open the roll to see your prints.
  5. SaveTap Save to put the print in Photos.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Graino has no account or login, and all processing happens on your iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Nothing is sent to a server, so cameras, rolls and the lab work without a connection.

Is Pocket 03 free?

Yes. Pocket 03, Sunny Toy and Square Pop are free and fully working.

Can I turn the date off?

Yes, the date stamp is a toggle.

Does the date follow my region?

The order follows your locale; year-first for Korean, Japanese and Chinese.

Can I add the CCD look to old photos?

Yes. Develop photos from your library, one at a time for free or up to 50 at once with Pro.

How many shots does the digital camera hold?

A card of 99, with instant review.

Does the viewfinder show the final look?

Yes, with the same rendering as the saved print.
