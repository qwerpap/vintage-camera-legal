How to Make iPhone Photos Look Like Film

Updated October 2026 · By the Graino developer

Short answer. Use a camera app that applies the film look while you shoot. In Graino you swipe to a camera body, press the shutter, and the saved print looks exactly like the live viewfinder.

Step by step

  1. Open Graino and pick a cameraOpen Graino and swipe the camera strip. Sunny Toy gives a warm toy-camera look, Mono 400 is black and white, Square Pop makes an instant square print.
  2. Frame the shotThe viewfinder already shows the finished film look. Use 0.5x ultra-wide if your iPhone has it, the front camera, or flash.
  3. Press the shutterEach shot is one frame of the roll (24 frames, 36 with Pro).
  4. Open the LabTap Lab at the top-left. Finished rolls develop in 60 minutes, or instantly with Pro.
  5. Open the roll and saveOpen the developed roll, pick a print and tap Save. It goes to Photos; you can also share or delete it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Graino has no account or login, and all processing happens on your iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Nothing is sent to a server, so cameras, rolls and the lab work without a connection.

Which camera should I start with?

Pocket 03, Sunny Toy and Square Pop are free and fully working.

Is the viewfinder the same as the saved photo?

Yes. The viewfinder uses the same rendering as the saved print.

Can I apply the look to photos I already took?

Yes. Develop photos from your library, one at a time for free or up to 50 at once with Pro.

How many cameras are there?

10 invented, hand-drawn camera bodies.

Does Graino record video?

No. Graino is for photos and prints.

How much does it cost?

Free to start. Pro is $3.99/month with a 3-day free trial or $29.99/year; there is no lifetime plan.
