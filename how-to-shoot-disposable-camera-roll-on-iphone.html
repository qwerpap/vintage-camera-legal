How to Shoot a Disposable-Camera Style Roll on iPhone and Develop It Later

Updated October 2026 · By the Graino developer

Short answer. Choose a film camera in Graino, shoot the roll without seeing the frames, then send it to the lab. A roll has 24 frames (36 with Pro) and develops in 60 minutes, or instantly with Pro.

Step by step

  1. Open Graino and pick a film cameraSwipe the strip to Sunny Toy or Paper Box for a disposable look.
  2. Shoot the rollPress the shutter for each frame. Frames stay hidden until the roll is developed.
  3. Finish or rewind the rollWhen the roll is finished or rewound it goes to the lab.
  4. Open LabTap Lab at the top-left and wait; development takes 60 minutes, or is instant with Pro.
  5. Open the roll and saveOpen the developed roll and tap Save on the prints you want.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Graino has no account or login, and all processing happens on your iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Nothing is sent to a server, so cameras, rolls and the lab work without a connection.

How many frames are in a roll?

24 on the free plan, 36 with Pro.

How long does developing take?

60 minutes, or instantly with Pro.

Can I see the photos before they are developed?

No. Frames stay hidden until the roll is developed.

Is Paper Box free?

No. The free cameras are Pocket 03, Sunny Toy and Square Pop; Paper Box needs Pro.

Do instant cameras also wait?

No. An instant print develops on screen in about 3 seconds, from a pack of 10.

Where are the prints saved?

In Photos, after you tap Save. Graino asks for add-only permission.
