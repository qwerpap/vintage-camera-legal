Graino Get the App

A film camera app for iPhone with 10 hand-drawn cameras, rolls that develop later, and a live film-look viewfinder.

Graino turns your iPhone into a set of film cameras. You pick a camera body on a strip, shoot with a viewfinder that already shows the finished film look, and the roll goes to a lab to develop. Everything is processed on the device: no account, no login, no server, and it works offline.

What Graino does

  • 10 invented, hand-drawn camerasPocket 03 (early-2000s CCD digicam with orange date), Sunny Toy (plastic toy film camera with warm light leaks), Square Pop (instant square print on white paper), Mono 400 (black and white), Night Flash (party flash with red halation), Slide 100 (slide film in a cardboard mount), Pano Wide (65:24 panorama), Paper Box (single-use cardboard camera look), Wide Print (wide instant print) and Dream Soft (soft-focus haze). You choose one by swiping a strip.
  • Viewfinder shows the real resultThe viewfinder renders the finished film look live, with the same rendering as the saved print, so the preview matches the result.
  • Rolls that develop in the labFilm cameras hold a roll of 24 frames (36 with Pro). When the roll is finished or rewound it goes to the lab and develops in 60 minutes (instantly with Pro). Frames stay hidden until they are developed.
  • Instant and digital camerasInstant cameras eject a print that develops on screen in about 3 seconds, from a pack of 10. The digital camera gives an instant review and a card of 99 shots.
  • Classic date stampA 7-segment orange date stamp that you can switch on or off. The order follows your locale; Korean, Japanese and Chinese settings put the year first (for example '26 10 22).
  • Camera options0.5x ultra-wide on iPhones that have it, front camera and flash.
  • Develop photos from your libraryTurn existing photos into film prints: one at a time for free, or up to 50 at once with Pro.
  • Private by designPrints are saved to Photos with add-only permission; you can also share or delete them. All processing happens on the device, with no account, no login and no server.

Price

Free: Pocket 03, Sunny Toy and Square Pop fully working, 24-frame rolls, the 60-minute lab and single-photo import. Graino Pro unlocks all 10 cameras, 36-frame rolls, instant developing and batch import of up to 50 photos. Subscription: $3.99/month (3-day free trial) or $29.99/year. There is no lifetime plan and there are no per-camera purchases.
