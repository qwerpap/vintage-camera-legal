How to Turn Photos From Your Library Into Film Prints on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Graino developer

Short answer. Open Graino, choose a camera, and develop photos from your library with its film look. It is free one photo at a time; Pro handles up to 50 at once.

Step by step

  1. Open Graino and pick a cameraSwipe the camera strip to the look you want, for example Mono 400 or Slide 100.
  2. Choose photos from your libraryUse the library import. Free users pick one photo at a time; Pro allows up to 50.
  3. Wait for the printsAll processing happens on your iPhone, so no upload is needed.
  4. Open Lab and the rollOpen Lab (top-left) and open the roll that holds your prints.
  5. SaveTap Save to add the prints to Photos, or share or delete them.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Graino has no account or login, and all processing happens on your iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Nothing is sent to a server, so cameras, rolls and the lab work without a connection.

How many photos can I develop at once?

One at a time for free, up to 50 with Pro.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on the device.

Does the saved print match the preview?

Yes. The preview and the saved print use the same rendering.

Which permission does Graino need to save?

Add-only access to Photos.

Do library photos get the date stamp?

The date stamp is a toggle you can switch on or off.

What does Pro cost?

$3.99/month with a 3-day free trial or $29.99/year; no lifetime plan.
