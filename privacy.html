Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Graino is a film camera app. It has no account, no login and no server of its own. This page explains exactly what happens to your data.

Camera

When you open the camera, the live picture is shown through the film look you chose. Frames are processed on your iPhone. A frame is stored only when you press the shutter, and it is kept inside the app on your device (in your rolls). Nothing from the camera is uploaded anywhere.

Photo library

Graino asks for add-only access to Photos, and only when you choose to save a print. It can add photos to your library; it cannot read or browse it. When you develop photos from your library, you pick them in the system photo picker — Graino receives only the photos you selected, and develops them on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store. To check whether Pro is active, the app uses Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc.) as a subscription processor. Adapty receives an anonymous app profile identifier, your App Store purchase and subscription status, the app version and basic device information (model, iOS version, locale, country of the App Store). Adapty does not receive your photos. See Adapty's privacy policy.

What we don't do

No tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifiers, no sale of data, no analytics besides the subscription processor above, no face recognition.

Deleting your data

Delete a print or a whole roll inside the app, or delete the app — all rolls are removed with it. Prints you saved to Photos stay in Photos. To delete Adapty's subscription record, write to us.

Children

Graino is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
